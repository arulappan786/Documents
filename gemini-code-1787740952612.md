### Objective
Refactor the customer validation and update flow by removing a side-effecting SOAP API call from a business rule validation step and moving it into the appropriate customer service update flow.

### Context & Problem
Currently, under the **"CustomerInput"** validation process, a business rule contains side-effecting logic that makes an external SOAP API call during validation:
- **RuleSetId:** 2
- **RuleId:** 28379
- **Target Logic:** Business Rule 2 (BR2), which invokes `CreateInvoiceMessageChangeRequests(@UtilityId, CustomerId, InvoiceMessage, @StoredInvoiceMessage)`.
- **Underlying Call:** `WebApiHelperServiceClient.CreateInvoiceMessageChangeRequests(...)`.

Validation should be side-effect free. We need to decouple this SOAP call from validation and move the invocation and its condition checks to the proper service layer.
